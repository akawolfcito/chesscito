## 0. The one finding that reframes the brief

The brief asks whether the mini-games are *unpopular* or *buried*. Production answers
it without ambiguity, and the answer is neither of the two shapes the brief expected.

| piece  | accounts that STARTED an exercise | accounts with ≥3 completed | accounts that COMPLETED ≥1 mini-game | mini-game conversion |
|--------|----------------------------------:|---------------------------:|-------------------------------------:|---------------------:|
| rook   | 939 | 602 | 552 | **91,7 %** |
| bishop |  97 |  72 |  70 | **97,2 %** |
| knight |  49 |  37 |  34 | **91,9 %** |
| queen  |  25 |  22 |  17 | **77,3 %** |
| pawn   |  17 |  16 |  13 | **81,3 %** |
| king   |  17 |  15 |  14 | **93,3 %** |

**Between 77 % and 97 % of every player who reaches a piece plays that piece's
mini-game.** The mini-games are not being refused. They are behind a wall that has
nothing to do with them:

```
777  accounts completed ≥1 rook exercise
290  completed ≥5
107  reached the rook badge gate (≥8 of 10 = 80 %)
103  fired badge_claim_tx        ← ON-CHAIN TRANSACTION
 97  ever started a bishop exercise
```

`badge-sheet.tsx:259` — `isPieceUnlocked(index)` returns true only when
`badgesClaimed[PIECES[index-1]]` is true. **Switching from the rook to the bishop
requires an on-chain badge claim.** That single gate costs 86 % of the population
(777 → 107) before any mini-game is even reachable.

So the addressable audience for each non-rook mini-game today is not "everyone" and
not "nobody" — it is **exactly the 97/49/25/17/17 people who cleared an on-chain
transaction.** A Mini-games surface that reads the *piece-local* gate instead of the
badge chain multiplies that audience by **8× for the bishop, 16× for the knight,
31× for the queen, 45× for the pawn and the king** — with no new content, no new
art and no payment.

That is the whole thesis of this spec. Everything below serves it.

> ⚠️ **DISCOVERY vs QUALITY — where the evidence CANNOT separate them.**
> There is no `minigame_started`, no `special_training_opened`, no "reached the lane"
> event anywhere in the codebase. The only mini-game event that exists is
> `labyrinth_complete`. Every "conversion" number above is
> *completed-mini-game / completed-3-exercises*, i.e. a **lower bound on interest**:
> a player who opened a mini-game and quit is invisible. The numbers can prove the
> games are good enough to finish; they cannot measure abandonment. Slice B must ship
> `minigame_start` before any quality judgement is made about a specific game.

## PART 1 — Inventory of current Learn content

### 1.1 How the catalog is actually shaped

Two independent pools, both content-sourced and regenerated by `pnpm import-puzzles`:

| pool | file | runtime constant | rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exercises (lane 1) | `apps/web/content/exercises.json` | `EXERCISES` | 60 (10 × 6 pieces) |
| Special Training (lane 2) | `apps/web/content/labyrinths.json` | `LABYRINTHS` + 5 signature pools | 34 |

The 34 lane-2 rows carry a `kind` discriminator and are split into six runtime
buckets (`lib/game/exercises.ts:97-195`). `projectSpecialTrainingLane`
(`lib/training/special-training-lane.ts`) then REPLACES a piece's raw labyrinths with
its signature pool where one exists. What ships per piece today:

| piece | shipped lane | kind | levels | replaced (retired, still in content) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| rook   | **Rook Rail**       | `labyrinth`      | 4 | — (rook's signature game *is* its labyrinths) |
| bishop | **Pivot Run**       | `diagonal-run`   | 3 | `bishop-lab-3`, `-4` |
| knight | **Knight's Tour**   | `knight-tour`    | 3 | `knight-lab-1..5` |
| queen  | **N-Queens**        | `queens`         | 3 | `queen-lab-1..3` |
| king   | **Safe Path**       | `safe-path`      | 3 | `king-lab-1` |
| pawn   | **Promotion Run**   | `promotion-run`  | 3 | `pawn-lab-1,3,4,5` |

Retired ids are preserved by `RETIRED_LANE_IDS` (`lib/training/retired-lane.ts`) for
one purpose only: the mastery crown is never revoked by an internal id change.

### 1.2 Classification

| item | classification | why |
|---|---|---|
| `EXERCISES` (60 baseline, cap 100/piece) | **EXERCISE** | single-target or sweep, star-graded via `computeStars`, feeds `progress.stars` and the score |
| Rook Rail (4) | **MINIGAME** | obstacle-maze, move-count graded, does not feed `progress.stars` |
| Pivot Run (3) | **MINIGAME** | turn-based pivot game, own board component |
| Knight's Tour (3) | **HYBRID / NOT READY** | coverage-graded AND `starless` — awards no stars at all |
| N-Queens (3) | **MINIGAME** | coverage-graded placement puzzle, own board |
| Safe Path (3) | **MINIGAME** | first losable lane-2 game, arrival-graded |
| Promotion Run (3) | **NOT READY** | failure-graded; `optimalMoves` "GRADES NOTHING" per its own source comment |
| `bishop-pivot-*`, `*-lab-*` | **retired** | out of the lane, kept only for the crown |

### 1.3 Per-candidate detail

Each row's *position* is the same for all of them: they are **interleaved into the
exercise list** by `interleaveTrainingRows` (`lib/training/path.ts`), producing
`Ex → Ex → Ex → Lab → Ex → Lab → …`. That interleave is exactly the structure the
brief wants dissolved.

**ROOK RAIL** — ids `rook-rail-two-turns | -dead-end | -rook-run | -two-roads`
- Gate: `LABYRINTH_UNLOCK_THRESHOLD = 6` stars **AND** `LABYRINTH_MIN_EXERCISES = 3`; levels 2-4 chain on the previous one.
- Mechanic: friendly blockers force a non-trivial route; multi-target rows are Star Sweeps (`sweep-optimal.ts`).
- Completion: reach target(s). Stars = `labyrinthStars(moves, optimalMoves)`; 0★ allowed, never a fail.
- Replayability: 1,10–1,41 completions/account. Low but non-zero.
- Production readiness: **SHIPPED, healthy.** 547 → 339 → 181 → 135 accounts across the four levels — the gentlest internal decay of any lane.
- Stands alone outside the path: **yes**, with one caveat — it is the piece everyone already has, so its marginal value on a new surface is replay, not discovery.

**PIVOT RUN (bishop)** — `bishop-run-1..3`
- Gate: same compound gate on the bishop's own stars, then chained.
- Mechanic: turn-based pivot (`lib/game/diagonal-run.ts`), board `diagonal-run-board.tsx`, dev fixture `/dev/diagonal-run`.
- Replayability 1,09–1,39. Internal decay 69 → 68 → 64 accounts: **essentially flat, the best retention in the catalog.**
- Readiness: **SHIPPED, healthy. Best candidate after the rook.**
- Stands alone: **yes.**

**KNIGHT'S TOUR** — `knight-tour-1..3`
- Mechanic: coverage tour. Graded by `tourStars`, **and listed in `starlessLaneIds`** — completion is preserved, stars are always 0 (`path.ts:157-163`).
- Internal decay **34 → 1 → 2 accounts.** The gate was open for all 34 (level 2 unlocks on level 1 complete). A 97 % cliff with the door open.
- Readiness: **NOT READY as a 3-level game.** Two independent defects: a level-2 cliff, and a card that can never show a score because the lane awards none.
- Stands alone: level 1 only, and only after the cliff is diagnosed.

**N-QUEENS (queen)** — `queens-1..3`
- Mechanic: placement puzzle, coverage-graded (`optimalMoves` = queens the player places, denominator `optimalMoves + 1`).
- Internal decay **17 → 17 → 17 accounts. Zero.** Every player who started finished all three.
- Replayability 1,06–1,29.
- Readiness: **SHIPPED, healthy — confirms the founder's read that the Queen game is one of the strongest.** Caveat: n = 17.
- Stands alone: **yes.**

**SAFE PATH (king)** — `king-safe-1..3`
- Mechanic: first losable lane-2 game; arrival-graded by move count (feeds `labyrinthStars`, NOT `tourStars`).
- Internal decay **13 → 14 → 13.** Flat.
- Readiness: **SHIPPED, healthy.** n = 13-14.
- Stands alone: **yes.** ⚠️ contradicts the brief's assumption that the King "may not have a meaningful mini-game yet" — it does, and it retains.

**PROMOTION RUN (pawn)** — `pawn-promotion-1..3`
- Mechanic: failure-graded run to promotion.
- Internal decay **13 → 6 → 1.**
- Replayability 3,31 / 5,50 / 4,00 completions per account — by far the highest, but on n = 13/6/1 that is a handful of enthusiasts, not a signal.
- ⛔ Its own source comment: *"`optimalMoves` is here because every level carries it, but it GRADES NOTHING"* — every winning run from rank r measures exactly `7 − r`.
- Readiness: **NOT READY.** Confirms the founder's read. Do not build a replacement in this pass.

## PART 3 — Proposed Learn information architecture

### 3.1 What Learn Home is today

`learn-hub-client.tsx` → `HubLiteScaffold`:

1. HUD (Peones chip, trophies, account, daily corner-trigger)
2. Mascot
3. `ChallengeCard` — the Season Pass / Focus Passport / primary CTA
4. `MasteryDashboard` — **six piece tiles** (`deriveRewardTiles`)

Mini-games are **not on this screen at all**. They exist only as interleaved rows
inside the `/exercises` drawer, behind a per-piece gate, behind the badge chain.

### 3.2 Proposed structure

```
LEARN

  [HUD: Peones · trophies · account · daily]

  1. CONTINUE LEARNING
       current piece · progress · next exercise      → /exercises?piece=<current>

  2. MINI-GAMES                                       → new section, in-place
       [Rook Rail] [Pivot Run] [N-Queens] [Safe Path]
       horizontal scroller, 4 cards

  3. DAILY  (existing corner trigger + passport — unchanged)

  4. PIECES  (compact strip, 6 icons + state — replaces the six tiles)
```

### 3.3 Should the six piece cards stay? — **No, not as cards.** Evidence:

- 939 accounts reached the rook; **97 ever reached the bishop.** For ~90 % of the
  population, five of the six tiles are permanently `locked-buildable` — dead pixels
  on a 390 px viewport.
- The tiles are pure navigation. Badge *claiming* does not live here
  (`[[project_badge_claim_lives_in_the_drawer_not_the_hub]]` — a `claimable` tile only
  routes to `/exercises`), so collapsing them costs no monetized action.
- `hub_reward_tile_tap` already carries `{piece, state}`, so the change is measurable
  against its own baseline.

**Recommendation — the smallest model that stays honest**: **current piece only** as
a full card, plus a **6-dot progress strip** (icon + `claimed | done | current |
locked`) that expands to the existing `MasteryDashboard` on tap. Reasons: "current +
next" implies a next the badge chain will not actually give them; "all pieces" is
what we have and it is 5/6 dead.

### 3.4 What NOT to do

Do not make Mini-games a new route. `/exercises` already owns every board component,
the drawer, `requestTrainingContent`, the access resolver and the completion overlays.
A second route would fork all of it. The section links **into** `/exercises` via the
existing `?content=` deep link (see 5.4).

## PART 4 — Exercises path after the separation

### 4.1 How the current piece is chosen

`selectPrimaryPiece(PLAYABLE_PIECES, paths)` (`lib/hub/content-loop.ts:261`) returns
the first piece in `["rook","bishop","knight","pawn","queen","king"]` with work left.
It is not pinned to the rook any more, but in practice it always returns the rook
because the rook is never exhausted before the player leaves.

`deriveRewardTiles` uses a *different* order (`REWARD_TILE_ORDER` = rook, bishop,
queen, knight, pawn, king). ⚠️ **The two orders disagree.** Not a bug today (the
tiles only route), but if the Mini-games section derives ordering from either, pick
one and pin it in a test.

### 4.2 Advancement — unchanged

- Exercise nodes are always `available` (`unlock: {type:"always"}`), status flips to `complete` at ≥1★.
- Badge node unlocks at `badgeRequiredCount(pool) = ceil(0.8 × pool)` distinct completions.
- Mastery node = badge claimed **AND** every lane level complete (or `retiredLaneComplete`).

### 4.3 Are special games removed from the linear sequence? — **Yes, from the SEQUENCE. No, from the PATH.**

Concretely: **`interleaveTrainingRows` stops being called for drawer presentation.**
That is the entire change. It is presentation-only by its own contract
(*"this orders rows, it never gates them"*), so removing it:

- does not touch `buildTrainingPath`,
- does not touch any unlock rule,
- does not touch mastery,
- does not touch stored progress.

The drawer renders `Ex → Ex → Ex → … → Badge → Mastery`. Lane-2 nodes remain in the
returned `TrainingNode[]` (mastery still reads them) but render in the Mini-games
section instead of between exercises.

Two functions become dead for the drawer and must be handled explicitly rather than
left dangling:

- `nextPendingLabyrinthAfterExercise` and `getLabyrinthForAutoAdvance` — the
  auto-advance *into* a mini-game after an exercise. **Recommendation: keep the
  auto-advance for the CURRENT piece only, one time, as the discovery bridge**
  (it is how 92 % of rook players found Rook Rail). Delete it and the free discovery
  path disappears with the interleave.
- `resolvePostLabContinue` — unchanged; still resolves the CTA after a mini-game.

### 4.4 Users who already completed content

Nothing changes for them. Stars are keyed by exercise id and immune to reordering;
lane bests are keyed by level id in a separate map. See PART 12.

### 4.5 Locked pieces

⛔ The badge-chain lock (`isPieceUnlocked`, on-chain claim required) is **out of scope
for the exercises path in this pass** — changing it is a separate product decision
with an on-chain dependency. What this spec changes is that **the Mini-games surface
does not consult it.** The exercise path keeps its current lock exactly as is.

### 4.6 Migration of ids or persisted progress — **none.** No id changes, no key changes, no rewrites.

wanted: Rook, Bishop, Queen.

No new Pawn or King mini-game is to be built in this pass, per the brief.

(`app/[locale]/exercises/page.tsx`), resolved by `requestTrainingContent`, the single

function, no new route, no new state.

## PART 6 — Access model

### 6.1 Hard rule honoured

Nothing that is free today becomes paid. Every mini-game stays reachable at zero cost
through progression. Peones buy **earliness**, never access.

### 6.2 Two gates exist today — only one of them should survive on this surface

| gate | where | keep on Mini-games? |
|---|---|---|
| piece-local: `totalStars ≥ 6` **AND** `completedExercises ≥ 3` in that piece | `path.ts:113` `meetsFirstLabGate` | **YES** — it is pedagogically real |
| piece-chain: previous piece's badge **claimed on-chain** | `badge-sheet.tsx:259` | **NO** — this is Wall B |

**This is the highest-leverage line in the spec.** Dropping the chain for the
Mini-games surface costs one condition and buys an 8–45× audience. It requires no
migration, no payment and no content: the piece-local gate is already computed
per-piece, independently, by `buildTrainingPath`.

### 6.3 Normal (free) unlock — per mini-game

```
Mini-game(piece P) is AVAILABLE  ⟺  ≥3 exercises completed in P  AND  ≥6 stars in P
Level k>1                        ⟺  level k−1 complete           (existing chain, unchanged)
```

Rook Rail is free for anyone who has done 3 rook exercises — i.e. 604 of the 778
active learners, on day 1.

### 6.4 Early unlock — per mini-game, with Peones

```
Spend N Peones  →  the piece-local gate for that ONE mini-game is permanently satisfied
```

It bypasses **only** the 3-exercise/6-star floor. It does **not** bypass the intra-lane
chain (level 2 still needs level 1) — buying past a chain would sell the player a level
they cannot play.

Scope: **one purchase covers that piece's whole lane** (all 3-4 levels), not one level.
Per-level pricing turns a 4-level game into a 4× toll and reads as pay-to-play.

### 6.5 State model

The unlock is an entitlement **derived from the ledger**, never a new table:

```
unlocked_early(wallet, piece) ⟺ ∃ peones_ledger row
    event_type = 'spend' AND source = 'minigame_unlock' AND source_id = <piece>
```

Cross-device for free, idempotent through the existing `idempotency_key` unique index,
auditable by the same queries used above.

## PART 7 — Peones spend model for mini-games

### 7.1 PERMANENT_UNLOCK. Confirmed, and it is also what the code wants.

Per-attempt charging would need a per-attempt idempotency key and a retry-priced loop
— the exact `fail → pay → fail → pay` shape the brief rejects. Permanent unlock needs
one key per `(wallet, piece)` and never fires again.

### 7.2 Coach stays what it is

`SPEND_COST_BY_TARGET` (`lib/peones/spend-service.ts:52`), Economy V1 2026-07-21:

| target | cost | model |
|---|---:|---|
| coach | 10 | consumable, per analysis (the only sink with real per-call LLM cost) |
| shield | 5 | consumable, saves one exercise |
| hint | 2 | consumable |
| **minigame_unlock** | **5 (proposed)** | **permanent, per piece** |

### 7.3 Price — flat 5 Peones for every mini-game

⛔ **Read this before arguing for a price ladder.** Production balances, all-time:

| wallets with a ledger row | median balance | p90 | ≥5 Peones | ≥10 | ≥15 | ≥25 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6.477 | **1** | 2 | **48** | 27 | 21 | 15 |

**0,74 % of wallets could afford a 5-Peón unlock today.** At 10 Peones it is 0,42 %.
A 5/10/15 ladder is not a pricing decision at these balances — it is three
indistinguishable ways of saying "top up first". With ~306 weekly active accounts,
no ladder is separable. **Flat 5.** It is also exactly the top-up minimum, so the
blocked → paid path is one $0,05 purchase with no arithmetic for the player.

### 7.4 ⚠️ What this means for hypothesis H2

The brief wants to learn *"do users spend earned Peones at all?"*. With a median
earned balance of 1 and a daily cap of 3 (`PEONES_DAILY_CAP`), **H2 is close to
untestable as stated**: the currency to spend does not exist. Lifetime totals:

- earned: welcome_pack 6.471 · daily_tactic 1.504 · exercise_completion 547 · pack_purchase 1.100
- spent: hint 251 (97 wallets) · coach 97 (**2 wallets**) · shield 52 (4 wallets)

H2 should be reframed as: **"when a player is one top-up away from a specific thing
they want, do they cross?"** — which is H3. Report H2 honestly as a secondary read on
the 48 wallets that can act on it.

### 8.3 ⛔ One trap to record

`app/api/peones/earn/route.ts:50` — `MAX_SINGLE_EVENT_AMOUNT = 50`. Pack credit does
**not** go through that endpoint (it goes through `verify-payment` →
`consume_legacy_get_peones_payment`), so a 100-Peón pack settles fine today. Any future
refactor that routes pack credit through `/api/peones/earn` would silently cap every
purchase at 50 and return HTTP 200.

### 8.4 Proposed model

```
min      5      ($0.05)
step     5      ($0.05)
default  25     ($0.25)
max      100    ($1.00)
price    peones × 10_000n USD6      (1 Peón = $0.01, exact)
```

**Why a max, and why 100.** A max is needed for two reasons: `PEONES_PACKS` is a
finite record (20 generated SKUs, `peones_pack_5 … peones_pack_100`), and an unbounded
selector lets a player buy a balance with no sink — total lifetime spend across **all**
wallets is 400 Peones. 100 also keeps the ladder from colliding with the two products
above it: Season Pass $0,99 and PRO $1,99. A $1,00 top-up must never be a worse deal
than the Season Pass.

**One parameterized card, not 20 SKU cards.** The UI holds one `amount` state; peones,
price label and the payment request all derive from it. `getPeonesPack(sku)` stays the
single source of truth so the shown price can never drift from the charged one — the
same discipline `SPEND_COST_BY_TARGET` already enforces for sinks.

## PART 9 — Contextual top-up

### 9.1 There is already a precedent — reuse it, do not invent

`result-overlay.tsx:63` already carries a *"Recovery CTA for an error variant (e.g.
insufficient Peones → Get Peones)"*, and `exercises-screen.tsx:680` already mounts a
`GetPeonesSheet` opened from the insufficient-save overlay. `ChesitoCard` (the Peones
chip modal) already has a Top-up CTA into the same sheet.

**One mechanism. `GetPeonesSheet`, parameterized by `amount` and by an `entry` prop for
telemetry.** No second payment flow, no second rail, no second verifier.

### 9.2 Two contexts to wire

| context | trigger | preselect |
|---|---|---|
| Coach with insufficient balance | `/api/peones/spend` → `insufficient_balance` (cost 10) | `roundUpToStep(10 − balance)` |
| Mini-game early unlock, insufficient balance | client-side check against cost 5 | `roundUpToStep(5 − balance)` |

`roundUpToStep(missing) = max(5, ceil(missing / 5) × 5)`

### 9.3 Preselect rule — **minimum missing, rounded to step.** Recommended.

**Tradeoff, stated plainly.**

*Minimum missing (recommended).* The hypothesis under test is whether a **low** ticket
converts at all. With a median balance of 1, a blocked player needs 4 Peones = $0,05.
Preselecting 25 asks for **5× what they need at the exact moment they are blocked**,
which tests price tolerance instead of the thing we want to learn. Cost: lower revenue
per conversion, and repeat purchases each cost a MiniPay transaction plus gas.

*Default 25.* Higher revenue per conversion, fewer transactions, and it leaves a
balance behind that can be spent again — which is the only way H2 ever becomes
testable. Cost: it converts the "$0,05 experiment" into a "$0,25 experiment", and the
brief's own framing ("many users have some stablecoin but not enough for PRO at
$1,99") argues the threshold is what matters.

**Resolution**: preselect the minimum, keep the `[−] [+]` prominent, and label it
*"covers this unlock"*. The `topup_amount_changed` event then measures whether players
voluntarily step up — which answers the default question with data instead of a guess,
and the default can be moved in one constant afterwards.

Entry from the Shop (not blocked, no missing amount) keeps **default 25**.

## PART 10 — The economic loop

### 10.1 Verified earning rules (from code, not memory)

| source | rule | where |
|---|---|---|
| `welcome_pack` | **+1, once per wallet**, written server-side by the balance route | `welcome-pack-server.ts:62` |
| `daily_tactic` | **+1 per UTC day** | earn route allow-list |

| daily cap | **3/day** across the daily family | `PEONES_DAILY_CAP`, mirrored in SQL |

⛔ Labyrinths and lane-2 games **pay nothing** (Economy V1, 2026-07-21). Mini-games
must not become a faucet — that decision stands and this spec does not touch it.

### 10.2 The loop

```
EARN                                  SPEND
  welcome pack        +1 once           hint              2   consumable
  daily tactic        +1 / day          shield            5   consumable
  5 exercises         +1 / milestone    minigame unlock   5   PERMANENT  (new)
  ── capped at 3/day ──                 coach            10   consumable
                    ↓                                  ↑
                  PEONES  ──────────────────────────────
                    ↑
  OPTIONAL TOP-UP: stablecoin → Peones, $0.01/Peón, 5…100
```

### 10.3 The honest arithmetic

A committed free player earns 1/day from the Daily plus 1 per 5 exercises, capped at 3.
Realistic steady state: **1-2 Peones/day.** A 5-Peón unlock is therefore **3-5 days of
play, or $0,05.** That is a defensible convenience price *only because* the same game
is free through progression — which it is (PART 6.3).

⚠️ Ceiling check: the full catalog gives ~15 milestone Peones lifetime from exercises.
Six mini-game unlocks at 5 = 30. **The free economy cannot buy all six early unlocks**,
by design — that is what makes it a convenience purchase rather than a grind gate.

## PART 11 — PRO interaction

### 11.1 What PRO does to Peones today

`PRO_BYPASS_DAILY_QUOTA` (`lib/peones/pro-bypass.ts:36`):

```
coach:   5 / day      hint:  20 / day      shield:  0 / day
```

PRO does not grant Peones. It grants a **daily allowance of forgiven spends** on
consumables: the ledger writes a `spend` row with `pro_bypass = true`, `debited = 0`,
and `peones_balance_with_caps` excludes it (Economy V1 restored that clause).

### 11.2 Recommendation: **B — PRO does NOT affect mini-game unlocks.**

Chosen on product semantics and simplicity, not generosity:

1. **The mechanism does not fit.** `pro_bypass` is a *per-day quota on a repeated
   action*. A permanent unlock happens once. "5 permanent unlocks per day" is
   meaningless, and "unlimited" (`!Number.isFinite(limit)`) would grant all six on a
   $1,99 subscription for content that is free anyway.
2. **Expiry has no correct answer.** If PRO granted the unlock, does it revoke on
   lapse? Revoking reads as theft; not revoking makes PRO a one-month permanent-unlock
   sale. Both are worse than not granting it.
3. **PRO adds nothing the player lacks.** Mini-games are free through progression.
   PRO's value is Coach and its bypass quota — untouched.
4. **`shield: 0` is the precedent.** The codebase already ships a Peones sink PRO does
   not cover, deliberately, with the note *"no PRO entitlement decided yet… easy to
   raise later"*. Mini-game unlock joins it at 0.

Not changed: PRO price, duration, purchase flow, bypass quotas, Coach semantics.

### 12.3 Migration semantics

**No destructive migration. Derive, never rewrite.** Unlock state is the OR of three
independent sources:

```
unlocked(wallet, piece) =
      localBest(piece) has any recorded level              -- device, instant
   OR ∃ score_attempts(wallet, exercise_id ∈ lane(piece))  -- cross-device
   OR meetsFirstLabGate(piece)                             -- earned now
   OR ∃ ledger spend(minigame_unlock, source_id = piece)   -- purchased
```

| user | outcome |
|---|---|
| already completed a mini-game | **unlocked** (two independent sources agree) |
| already reached its prerequisite | **unlocked** — the gate is recomputed, not stored |
| halfway through a piece | unchanged; gate recomputes from live stars |
| completed later pieces | unchanged; each piece's gate is independent (this is what removing the chain buys them) |
| restores on another device | **unlocked** via `score_attempts` + ledger — strictly better than today, where localStorage loss loses everything |
| never played | free path unchanged, early unlock offered |

One additive migration:

alter table public.peones_ledger add constraint <source_check>
  check (source in (…existing…, 'minigame_unlock'));

## PART 14 — Mini-game card states

Six states, no ambiguous "locked":

| state | condition | CTA | secondary line |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AVAILABLE` | gate met, never completed | **Play** | — |
| `IN_PROGRESS` | ≥1 level complete, not all | **Continue** | `2/4 levels` |
| `COMPLETED` | every level complete | **Play again** | `★ 9/12` (or `4/4 levels` for starless lanes) |
| `EARLY_UNLOCK_AVAILABLE` | gate not met, balance ≥ 5 | **Unlock · 5 ₱** | `or complete 3 Rook exercises` |
| `PROGRESSION_LOCKED` | gate not met, balance < 5 | **Complete 3 Rook exercises** | `or unlock now · 5 ₱ →` (opens contextual top-up) |
| `COMING_SOON` | Knight's Tour, Promotion Run | *(inert)* | `Coming soon` — **no Peones affordance at all** |

Rules:
1. Every locked card **names its free path**. A lock that only offers a price is the
   pay-to-learn read the brief forbids.
2. `PROGRESSION_LOCKED` and `EARLY_UNLOCK_AVAILABLE` differ only by affordability, so
   the copy is identical and only the affordance changes — a player is never told two
   different stories about the same wall.
3. `COMING_SOON` accepts no taps and shows no price. Never sell an unfinished game.
4. ⛔ **`COMPLETED` for a starless lane must not render an empty star slot.** If
   Knight's Tour ever ships, it needs a coverage figure, not a `★` — its lane awards
   none (`starlessLaneIds`). This is the second reason it is held back.
5. ⚠️ Card state is a **DOM assertion**, never a VR baseline. A card badge is ~450 px²
   against `hub-clean`'s ~1.646 px tolerance — 3,7× the element. A green VR run proves
   nothing about these chips.

## PART 16 — Success metrics

⚠️ **Sizing first.** Weekly active accounts: 5.351 (wk 2026-08-03, a listing spike) →
934 → **306**. Assume ~300/week. This supports directional reads, not A/B tests.
Do not design a split test at this population.

**H1 — DISCOVERY.** *When mini-games are visible directly, do users play them?*
- primary: `minigames_open` → `minigame_start` (session-level rate)
- **the decisive read**: distinct accounts starting a **non-rook** mini-game per week.
  Baseline from this audit: bishop 70, knight 34, queen 17, king 14 accounts in
  **15 weeks**. Any weekly number above ~5 non-rook accounts is a real move.
- guardrail: `exercise_complete` per active account must not fall. If mini-games
  cannibalise the exercise path, the IA change is not a win.

**H2 — PEONES UTILITY.** *Do users spend earned Peones on content?*
- `minigame_unlock_prompt` → `minigame_unlock_spent`, **restricted to prompts where
  `balance ≥ price`** — the only honest denominator.
- ⚠️ Report the eligible population beside the rate. It is **48 wallets today**. A rate
  computed over all 6.477 is meaningless.

**H3 — LOW-TICKET PAYMENT.** *Do users top up when Peones are insufficient?*
- `topup_open` → `topup_requested` → `topup_settled{confirmed}` → a subsequent
  `peones_spent`.
- baseline to beat: **22 purchases / 14 wallets / lifetime**, all at $0,50.
- the number that decides the experiment: **distinct wallets making a first-ever
  purchase at ≤ $0,25.** Not revenue — the lifetime revenue of this rail is $11,00 and
  a revenue read at that scale is noise.
- secondary: `topup_amount_changed` distribution → whether players step above the
  preselected minimum.

## PART 20 — Final deliverable

**CURRENT LEARN STRUCTURE:**
Hub (mascot · ChallengeCard · six piece tiles · daily corner trigger) → `/exercises`,
where lane-1 exercises and lane-2 mini-games are **interleaved into one list**
(`interleaveTrainingRows`) behind a per-piece gate (3 exercises + 6 stars), which is
itself behind a **piece chain that requires an on-chain badge claim** to advance from
one piece to the next.

**PROPOSED LEARN STRUCTURE:**
EXERCISES + MINI-GAMES — two sibling destinations on Learn Home. Exercises =
`Ex → Ex → Ex → Badge → Mastery`, uninterleaved. Mini-games = a parallel section
gated per piece **only** by that piece's own local gate, with the badge chain removed
for this surface.

**INITIAL MINI-GAMES:**
1. Rook Rail (rook, 4 levels)
2. Pivot Run (bishop, 3)
3. N-Queens (queen, 3)
4. Safe Path (king, 3)

**MINI-GAMES NOT READY:**
- Knight's Tour — 34 → 1 → 2 accounts across its three levels with the unlock open; also `starless`, so a completed card has no score to show. *(Contradicts the brief's Rook/Bishop/Knight proposal — recommend Queen + King instead.)*
- Promotion Run (pawn) — 13 → 6 → 1; `optimalMoves` grades nothing by its own source comment.

**NORMAL UNLOCK MODEL:**
Per piece: ≥3 exercises completed AND ≥6 stars in that piece (existing
`meetsFirstLabGate`). Level k>1 requires level k−1 (existing chain). **The
previous-piece on-chain badge claim is NOT consulted on this surface.**

**EARLY UNLOCK MODEL:**
Spend Peones to permanently satisfy one mini-game's piece-local entry gate. Does not
bypass the intra-lane chain. One purchase covers that piece's whole lane. Derived
from a ledger row; no new table.

**MINI-GAME PEONES MODEL:**
PERMANENT_UNLOCK — 5 Peones flat, every game. (Flat because only 48 of 6.477 wallets
hold ≥5 Peones; a 5/10/15 ladder is not separable at ~300 weekly actives.)

**TOP-UP MODEL:**
min 5 · step 5 · default 25 · max 100

**PRICE RULE:**
1 Peón = $0.01 exactly → `priceUsd6 = peones × 10_000n`. Twenty generated SKUs
`peones_pack_5 … peones_pack_100`; **one** parameterized card in the UI, single source
of truth `getPeonesPack(sku)`.

**COACH MODEL:**
Unchanged. 10 Peones per analysis, consumable, PRO bypass 5/day.

**PRO INTERACTION:**
**B — PRO does not affect mini-game unlocks.** PRO's Peones mechanism is a per-day
quota on repeated consumables; a permanent unlock has no per-day meaning, expiry has
no correct answer, and the games are free through progression. `shield: 0` is the
existing precedent. `PRO_BYPASS_DAILY_QUOTA.minigame_unlock = 0`.

**DATABASE MIGRATION REQUIRED:**
